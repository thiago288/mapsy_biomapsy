# Authors and contributors

I contributed to original MapSy as part of the MATERIALab Group at Boise State University. In this repository, I maintain my optimized version of MapSy and BioMapSy, the module I developed for aperiodic surfaces.

Original MapSy was developed within MATERIALab. The available project history records contributions from Oliviero Andreussi, Mulumba Denis, Jalen Lu, me (`thiago288`) and `zzayah`. This list may not include every contributor.

The original project is the [`mapsy` branch of BoiseState-MATERIALab/surface-maps](https://github.com/BoiseState-MATERIALab/surface-maps/tree/mapsy).
